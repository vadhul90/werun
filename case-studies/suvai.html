Suvai

A recipe app that helps people plan what to cook, with AI-generated meal plans, more than 5,000 recipes and grocery ordering built in.

The idea

Finding a recipe is easy. Deciding what to cook this week, and getting the ingredients, is the harder problem. Suvai was built to cover the whole path from plan to plate.

It combines a large recipe library with AI that plans meals, and connects the result to grocery ordering.

What we built

Inside Suvai.

AI-generated meal plans

Meal plans generated by AI, so users start from a plan rather than a blank week.

A library of 5,000+ recipes

A large catalog to browse and plan from.

Recipe collections

Users organize the recipes they want to keep into their own collections.

Instacart integration

Ingredients can be ordered through Instacart from inside the app.

Accounts

Sign-up with an email address or Apple Sign-In.

Subscriptions

A free tier with limited access, plus monthly and yearly Premium plans billed through the app stores.

Product decisions

The choices behind it.

Suvai is AI inside a shipped consumer product rather than a demo: generated meal plans, a third-party commerce integration, user accounts and subscription billing on both app stores.

  • AI is used for planning, where it saves the user real effort
  • A free tier with limited access, with Premium unlocking unlimited meal plans and the full library
  • Grocery ordering handled through an established partner integration
  • Built for both iOS and Android
