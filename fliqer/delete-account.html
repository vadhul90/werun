Delete Your Account

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

  • You can delete your Fliqer account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by email.
  • Deleting your account removes your sign-in, username, game results and leaderboard entries straight away.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Fliqer Pro subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store first.

1.Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Fliqer and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Tap your profile button in the top-right corner of the home screen.
  3. Tap Account settings.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again (with Google, Apple or your password) to prove it's you.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

2.Delete your account without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email Fliqer@werunconsulting.com from the email address linked to your Fliqer account, with the subject "Delete my Fliqer account". Include your Fliqer username if you have one. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we'll delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

3.What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: your email address, user ID, sign-in method and any name or profile details provided by Google or Apple.
  • Your profile: your username, free trial start date and Fliqer Pro status.
  • Your game data: every synced daily result, including guesses, picks, points, wins and streaks.
  • Your leaderboard entries: all-time, monthly and Critics' Score rows, across every mode.
  • Sign in with Apple: if you used it, we also revoke Fliqer's access to your Apple ID.

4.What is kept, and for how long

  • Backup copies: copies held by our service providers (Google Firebase) are removed within 60 days.
  • Finished Battle matches: 1v1 match records that include your username are deleted automatically within 2 days of the match.
  • Purchase records: if you bought Fliqer Pro, Google Play or the App Store and RevenueCat keep the transaction record (product, date, status, linked to an anonymous ID) as required for tax, accounting and refunds. It is not linked to your email by us.
  • Crash reports: crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Data on your device: settings, local stats and cached puzzles stay on your phone until you uninstall Fliqer or clear its data.
  • Anonymous totals: aggregated statistics, such as how many players solved a puzzle, no longer identify you and may be kept.

5.Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Fliqer Pro subscription, because billing is handled by the store. To cancel on Android, open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Fliqer. On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Fliqer.
