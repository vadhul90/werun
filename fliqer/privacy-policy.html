Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Guest progress stays on your device.
  • If you sign in, we store your email, your username and your game results so your progress syncs and you can appear on the leaderboards.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

1.Introduction

Fliqer, an offering of WeRun Consulting ("we", "us"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal information when you use the Fliqer mobile application ("App") on iOS and Android. It is written to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2.Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address, the sign-in method you used (Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or email and password) and a unique user ID. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's private relay address. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store them in plain text.
  • Username: The public display name you choose for the leaderboards.

Information created when you play

  • Game data: For signed-in players, we store the results of each daily puzzle across all four game modes (Stats Mode, Movie Trail, Greenlight and Battle Mode). This includes the puzzle date, your guesses or picks, points, wins, streaks and your Critics' Score. Guesses and picks are sent to our servers so the score can be checked against the real puzzle.
  • Free trial: The date your free trial started, so it can be applied to your account on any device.
  • Purchase information: Fliqer Pro purchases are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We receive your Pro status and basic transaction details, such as the product, purchase date and whether it is active. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Information from your device

  • Crash reports: If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report. It includes the device model, operating system and app version, technical details of the error, and an installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.
  • App integrity checks: Firebase App Check uses Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This protects the leaderboards from cheating and abuse.
  • Notifications: If you turn on daily reminders, your device registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging. It is placed in a broad regional reminder group (for example "Europe" or "Americas") worked out from your device's time zone, so the reminder arrives at a sensible hour. We do not use your location.
  • Data kept on your device: Settings, puzzle progress, stats, trophies, your Hall of Fame and cached daily puzzles are saved locally on your device so the App works quickly and offline. If you play as a guest, this is the only place your progress is stored.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not collect your device's advertising identifier. The App contains no advertising SDKs. When you share a result, it goes through your device's share sheet to the app you pick, and we don't receive a copy.

3.How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and secure your account and keep your progress in sync across devices.
  • Verify and score your daily results, and run the leaderboards, streaks, trophies and Critics' Score.
  • Apply your free trial and Fliqer Pro access.
  • Send the daily reminders you asked for.
  • Detect and prevent cheating, fraud and abuse.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App's stability and performance.
  • Respond to your support requests and tell you about important changes.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). We also rely on our legitimate interests in keeping the game fair, secure and working, and on your consent for push notifications. You can withdraw that consent at any time in the App or in your device settings.

5.Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information. These include encrypted connections (TLS), strict database security rules, and server-side validation of every score and username. App Check also blocks requests that don't come from the genuine App. No system is completely secure, so please keep your device and sign-in details safe.

6.Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to fix inaccurate information. You can change your username in the App.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and its data.
  • Objection and restriction: Object to, or ask us to limit, certain processing.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Australia, this is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Deleting your account: To delete your account, open the App, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your sign-in, profile, username, synced results and leaderboard entries. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the App's access to your Apple ID. You can also email us to request deletion.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Fliqer@werunconsulting.com. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires.

7.Data Retention

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database straight away. Any remaining copies held by our service providers are removed within 60 days, unless we must keep them for legal, tax or accounting reasons, such as purchase records.

Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Anonymised, aggregated statistics, such as how many players solved a puzzle, may be kept indefinitely because they no longer identify you.

8.International Data Transfers

Fliqer is operated from Australia. Your data is stored and processed in the United States by Google Firebase and RevenueCat, and may be processed in other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, these transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, in line with the GDPR and the Australian Privacy Principles.

9.Children's Privacy

Fliqer is not directed at children under 13 years old (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If we learn that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, contact us at Fliqer@werunconsulting.com.
