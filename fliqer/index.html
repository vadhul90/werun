Four ways to play

Each mode tests a different kind of movie knowledge, from box-office numbers to who worked with whom. All of them are built on a catalogue of 1,300 films.

STATS MODE

Stats Mode

Name the film from its stats.

  • Guess today's mystery film in 7 tries. Every guess fills seven boxes: genre, year, studio, director, leads, box office and rating.
  • Green is an exact match and yellow is close: the right parent studio, within 4 years, box office within 20%, or a director who has worked with the film's cast. Arrows tell you to go higher or lower.
  • Stuck after three guesses? Use one lifeline: a Trailer reveals a number, a Spoiler reveals a key credit, and a Retake gives you an extra eighth guess.
Up to 500 pts · fewer guesses, more points

MOVIE TRAIL

Movie Trail

Follow the clues. Person or film?

  • Each day the trail leads to an actor, director, composer or film. You start with one clue, and every wrong guess reveals the next.
  • Wrong guesses still help: Fliqer tells you when your guess worked with the answer, shares a director or played the same kind of role.
  • On rare Special Screening days, the trail leads to a famous character instead.

GREENLIGHT

Greenlight

Cast it. Pitch it. Get it made.

  • You're the producer. Every day the studio hands everyone the same slate: four directors, four leads, four co-stars, four genres and four decades.
  • Pick one of each and pitch your film. It works like fantasy football: points come from real film history, such as a director in their best genre, stars who have actually worked together and the right era.
  • Pitch a film that really exists for a bonus. Reach the day's target to get greenlit, then see the studio's own pick.
Up to 300 pts · 1,024 possible pitches a day

BATTLE MODE

Battle Mode

12 films. 6 questions. Outpick the AI or a friend.

  • You get the same 12 films and 6 questions as your opponent, such as "earlier release" or "bigger budget". Each round you both lock in a film, and the better answer wins.
  • Both films are then burned, so save the right film for the right question.
  • Play the AI at Matinee (easy) or Director's Cut (tough), or challenge a friend with an invite code. In 1v1 you pick at the same time, with 20 seconds a round.
Up to 700 pts · your first Battle of the day counts

House rules

Fair play and your privacy

Every score is checked

Your guesses and picks are scored again on our servers against the real puzzle, and only one result per mode per day counts. Puzzles stay locked until their release time, so nobody can peek ahead.

Your data stays yours

You can play as a guest without an account. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Questions

Frequently asked

When do new puzzles come out?

A new set of puzzles opens every day at midnight. Everyone plays the same puzzles on the same day, which is what makes the leaderboards and share cards fair.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start playing straight away as a guest, and your progress is saved on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google to pick a username, sync your stats between devices and appear on the leaderboards.

Which devices can I play on?

Fliqer is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android phones on Google Play. Sign in with the same account on both and your progress follows you.

What happens after the free trial?

For your first 7 days every mode is unlocked for free. After that, Fliqer Pro unlocks all four daily modes, the Vault, the leaderboards, trophies and Special Screenings. You can choose a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store or Google Play account.

Can I play a friend?

Yes. In Battle Mode, tap Challenge to get a six-letter invite code, then copy it or share it. Your friend enters it under Join with code, and you both play the same 12 films head to head. Codes last 30 minutes.

Where does the movie data come from?

Fliqer's catalogue of 1,300 films is built from Wikidata, Wikipedia and The Movie Database (TMDB). Ratings and genres come from TMDB. If you spot something that looks wrong, let us know and we'll fix it.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app and go to your profile → Account settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your sign-in, username, synced results and leaderboard entries.

How do I get in touch?

Email us at Fliqer@werunconsulting.com with questions, feedback, bug reports or puzzle ideas. We read everything.
