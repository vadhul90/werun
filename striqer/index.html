What is Striqer?

Striqer is a daily soccer trivia app available on iOS and Android. Each day brings a fresh puzzle across all four game modes. Whether you follow every match or just know the big names, there is something here for every level of fan.

Game Modes

STATS MODE

Study a player's career numbers and guess who it is in 7 attempts. Each correct stat narrows the field.

CLUB TRAIL

Trace a player's transfer history from club to club. How well do you know the moves?

REGISTA

Flip tiles to reveal higher/lower stats and piece together the mystery player from the clues.

DRAFT WAR

Pick your five-a-side squad and go head to head against the AI. Squad selection meets trivia.

Lifelines

Every player gets lifelines to use when they are stuck.

  • Throw-in – reveals a subtle stat clue
  • Penalty – reveals a key attribute such as nationality or position
  • VAR – adds an extra guess

Leaderboards

Sign in with Google to track your streaks, win rates, and compete on the global leaderboard across all four modes.

Player Data

Striqer features Premier League players and international all-time greats across Stats Mode, Club Trail, Regista, and Draft War. Player data is sourced from publicly available soccer records. Statistics are provided for entertainment purposes and may not always reflect the most current information.

Our Philosophy

Striqer is intentionally ad-free. The focus stays entirely on building something worth playing every day. We believe a great puzzle does not need ads or interruptions.
