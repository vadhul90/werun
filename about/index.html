About WeRun

Built as an EPM specialty.

Our story

WeRun Consulting started as a focused Oracle EPM Cloud practice - deep, senior expertise in enterprise planning, Essbase and BI. That same discipline now spans digital transformation, AI, mobile product development and cloud data engineering.

The practice changed. The standard didn't.

We're a boutique, Melbourne-based firm serving clients locally and remotely - small by design, so every engagement gets senior attention from strategy through to delivery.
